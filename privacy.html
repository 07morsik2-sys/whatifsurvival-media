Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

What If Survival Publisher ("the app") is used only by the owner of the What If Survival channel to publish the channel's own videos to the owner's own TikTok and Instagram accounts.

Data we access

When the owner signs in, the app receives an access token and basic account information (account ID and display name) for the owner's own account. This is used only to publish the owner's videos and to confirm that a post was published.

Data we store

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are never shared, sold or sent to anyone else. The app does not collect data about other users or viewers.

Deletion

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time in TikTok or Instagram settings; the stored token is then deleted.

Contact

Questions about privacy: whatifsurvival.ep on TikTok or Instagram.
